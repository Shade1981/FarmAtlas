FarmAtlas

Find where to farm every cooking meat, leather, scale, fish, ore and herb - right on your world map and minimap.

Made for WoW Forever. Search boar, raptor, stag or Giant Egg and FarmAtlas shows you the best farm areas with mob names, levels and drop chances, marks them on your map, tells you which recipes use the ingredient, and counts what you already have in your bags, bank and on your alts.

FarmAtlas is your farming and profession companion: where to farm it, what it is worth, what you can make from it and how to level your profession. For exact herb and ore nodes it works together with Questie, GatherMate2 and Gatherer - no duplicate maps.

Features

Farm atlas - 150+ farmable materials built in: cooking meats & ingredients, leather, hides & scales, fish, ores and herbs. - Every material lists its farm areas with mob names, level range and drop chance. - A recommended zone for your level, and dangerous zones are flagged. - Search by item, mob, zone or keyword (boar, wolf, rugged, squid...). - Materials are listed in leveling order, with the level or fishing skill on every row. - On me filter: only what is in your bags.

Map & minimap - Translucent, quest-style farm areas with a dashed outline on the world map. - Track several items at once, each in its own color. - Minimap: the area when it is close, an edge icon pointing the way when it is far, and a colored glow when you are standing inside it. - Fishing places sit on the water itself, one per lake / river / coast, with what you can catch there. - Herbs and ore at their exact nodes when Questie, GatherMate2 or Gatherer is installed. - Small icons where you (or your guild) actually gathered; adjustable size, auto-hide when you are close.

Leveling guides - Cooking, Fishing, Skinning, Mining, Herbalism, Blacksmithing, Engineering, Leatherworking, Tailoring, First Aid and Alchemy 1-300, each on its own tab: what to make, where to go, which rank to train next. - Click a step: the place is marked in gold on the world map; cooking steps show where to farm the material. - The trainers of your current step appear on the map and minimap automatically. - Town alert: a movable, resizable banner when you arrive at a town, village or camp with a trainer of a profession you switched on.

Recipes - Recipes tab: all 123 WoW Forever cooking recipes. The ones you don't know have a red veil, but the whole recipe is readable on hover - the game's own item tooltip with the exact effect, skill colors and reagents. - Click a known recipe to open your profession window on it; click an unknown one to see where to buy or learn it, with the place on the map. - Craftable now filter: only the recipes you have every ingredient for. - Reads your Cooking window automatically - no buttons.

Goals - Set how many you need (e.g. 20x Raptor Flesh). A progress bar counts your bags and bank, and you get an on-screen message and a sound when the goal is reached.

Bags, bank & alts - Item tooltips and the atlas show how many you have on this character, in the bank and on your alts.

Prices & farm / hour - Auction house price on item and mob tooltips from Auctionator, TSM or AHSnipe (all optional); the last known price, or the vendor price, when none is open. - Farm / hour in gold: what you really looted in the last 60 minutes and what it is worth, plus the value of everything you own.

Settings - Gear button in the atlas (or /farm config): every switch, grouped by topic. A short "What's new" window after each update.

Learns while you play - Every meat, hide, fish, ore or herb you loot or gather is saved with its exact spot and source (auto loot included). - Real WoW Forever drop rates from your own kills (e.g. Mottled Boar: 42% (21/50)).

Automatic sharing (no buttons) - Discoveries are shared in the background with your party, raid and guild members who also use FarmAtlas, through Blizzard's hidden addon channel (like GatherMate2). Lost your data (new PC, reinstall)? Guild and group members on 1.5.0+ send your own finds back at login. Turn sharing off with /farm sync.

Quick search - Alt + click any item (bags, chat link, recipe reagent) to open it in FarmAtlas.

Languages - English, Hungarian, French, German and Spanish interface. On a French, German or Spanish game client FarmAtlas starts in that language, and item, recipe and zone names are shown the way your game names them (English names work in the search too).

Help the community map

Drop chances and areas start from Classic-based data and get more precise with every player. Your WTF\Account\<ACCOUNT>\SavedVariables\FarmAtlas.lua file contains everything you and your guild discovered - share it with the author and it gets merged into the next release for everyone.
